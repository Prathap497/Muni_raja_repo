Why Prescribers Choose SUPER-HB

SUPER-HB addresses anemia with a highly absorbable iron form, minimizing the common gastrointestinal side effects associated with iron therapy. The addition of essential co-factors ensures comprehensive support for hemoglobin production.

How to Recommend

  • Suggest taking one capsule daily, ideally with a meal to further reduce gastric discomfort, though it is gentle enough for empty stomach.
  • Monitor hemoglobin levels periodically to assess response to therapy.

Why Prescribers Choose SNORIL-LX

SNORIL-LX combines the potency of a third-generation antihistamine with a leukotriene antagonist to provide complete coverage against allergic rhinitis. The addition of zinc and folic acid supports the repair of nasal mucosa damaged by chronic inflammation, reducing recurrence rates.

How to Recommend

  • Recommend one dose daily in the evening to maximize overnight symptom control and minimize any potential drowsiness.
  • Shake the bottle well before use to ensure uniform dosage of the suspension.
  • Advise completing the prescribed course even if symptoms subside early to prevent allergic rebound.

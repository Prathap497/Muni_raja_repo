Why Prescribers Choose FOLJOY

FOLJOY delivers active L-Methylfolate, bypassing the MTHFR polymorphism barrier that affects folic acid absorption in many patients. Combined with P5P and Methylcobalamin, it ensures robust neural tube protection and supports healthy red blood cell formation during critical prenatal windows.

How to Recommend

  • Advise one tablet daily, preferably after a meal, starting from preconception through the first trimester.
  • Highlight the pleasant orange flavor which helps improve compliance in patients with pregnancy-induced nausea.
  • Confirm patient is not taking other high-dose folic acid supplements to avoid redundancy.
